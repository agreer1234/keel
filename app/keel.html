    The method behind Keel

    Goal, Plan, System
    Every goal gets a clear outcome and a reason (Goal), 3–5 milestones (Plan), and scheduled habits plus someone you answer to (System).
    Seasons
    Work in 12-week seasons. Sprint on a few goals, keep the rest on Maintain or Pause, so your ambition fits the time you have.
    Time blocking and implementation intentions
    Each habit has a day, a time, a length and a cue ("After coffee, I will…"). A plan with a when and where is far more likely to happen.
    168 hours
    You get 168 hours a week. The weekly time view shows how many of them you planned for your goals and how many you used.
    Protected focus
    Start a focus session from any habit. Silence notifications on your phone and computer while it runs.
    Daily feedback loop
    A short evening check-in logs energy, a win, what got in the way and tomorrow's one thing. The coach reads it.
    Feel-good productivity
    The coach frames advice as Energise (play, power, people), Unblock (uncertainty, fear, inertia) or Sustain (rest and recovery).
